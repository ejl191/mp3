Infocom · 1980–1982 · Text adventures

Zork

The original Zork trilogy. Your place is saved after every move, so you can close it and pick up later.

How to play

Type what you want to do in plain English: open mailbox, take lamp, go north, put sword in case. The buttons above the keyboard cover directions and common commands.

Useful commands: look, inventory, score, verbose for full room descriptions, and again to repeat. save and restore keep one extra save slot per game.

Zork I, II and III were written by Tim Anderson, Marc Blank, Bruce Daniels and Dave Lebling. The story files are the originals from Microsoft's MIT-licensed source release (historicalsource on GitHub). Saves are kept on this device only.
